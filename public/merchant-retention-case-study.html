01The problem

Merchant support was reactive. By the time a merchant churned, the signals were months old.

Payment platforms and B2B SaaS lose 20–30% of merchants a year — not because the product failed, but because lifecycle management was reactive, generic, and too late. The same one-size message went to a thriving merchant and a dormant one.

High churn 20–30% / yr

Merchants left quietly. Intervention happened after the drop in volume, not before it.

Underutilized features

Merchants never discovered the capabilities that would have made them stick.

Missed expansion

Growing merchants hit plan limits with no proactive upsell — revenue left on the table.

One-size support

The same lifecycle journey for every merchant, regardless of stage, health, or behavior.

02The approach

Let an LLM read each merchant's signals and generate the playbook — per segment, on demand.

Behavioral and transaction signals feed an LLM, which classifies the merchant's lifecycle stage and generates a tailored, next-best-action playbook — instead of a static rulebook nobody maintains.

      04The shift

      From reacting to churn, to preventing it.

      Before Reactive

      • Intervention triggered after volume already dropped
      • Same generic lifecycle email for every merchant
      • Analysts hand-built playbooks that went stale
      • Expansion and win-back happened by luck, not design

      After Proactive

      • Early signals surface risk before the drop
      • Each merchant gets a playbook tuned to its stage
      • The model generates and refreshes playbooks on demand
      • Retention, expansion and win-back are designed surfaces
