      Why it was rebuilt

      A generator is a function. Retention needed a loop.

      Ran once, took no action

      It emitted a paragraph on request. Someone still had to read it and do the work.

      No memory

      It re-suggested tactics that had already failed on the same merchant last quarter.

      Never observed the outcome

      Nobody watched whether volume recovered, so nothing adjusted.

      No trust boundary

      No tiered autonomy, no guardrails, no gate on actions that touch money — because it never acted at all.
