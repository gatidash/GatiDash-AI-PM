# Removed claims

Every number, date and title on the site was checked against
`public/GatiDash_Resume.pdf`, then against git history, the existing site copy,
and public reporting where a claim touched something citable.

The rule: **never invent, estimate, round up, or infer a figure.** Where a
number appeared in no source, it was deleted and the sentence rewritten to work
without it. Where two sources disagreed, the more conservative one won. Two
further passes came out of the adversarial review — a hostile hiring manager and
an angel investor reading the built site cold — which caught claims that were
technically sourced but did not survive scrutiny.

Nothing here is a judgement about whether a claim is true. Only about whether the
sources on hand could stand behind it. **Restore any you can substantiate, and
put the evidence on the résumé so the next audit passes.**
